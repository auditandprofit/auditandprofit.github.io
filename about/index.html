About

Cloud Agents

A simple route from one prompt to one cloud agent.

The goal

Cloud Agents is building toward free unlimited DeepSeek V4.1 Flash tokens and unlimited cloud-agent runtime, with more models planned.

How the service works today

You submit a task through the Cloud Agents app. The app starts an isolated run, keeps its logs on a job page, and provides a finished-workspace archive when one is available. Individual runs currently last up to three hours and can be rerun.
